# Getting real material

Ask at most 3 questions in one message. Pick the ones whose answers are missing. Keep them short and concrete; people answer concrete questions fast.

## Scene questions
- 그때 누구랑 있었어요?
- 몇 시쯤, 어디였어요?
- 그 순간 누가 한 말 중에 기억나는 한마디 있어요?
- 눈에 보이던 것 하나만 말해주면? (숫자, 물건, 날씨)
- 결국 어떻게 끝났어요?

## Purpose questions
- 읽는 사람이 웃었으면 해요, 공감했으면 해요, 댓글 달았으면 해요?
- 이 계정은 주로 누가 봐요? (동료, 친구, 같은 업계, 모르는 사람)

## Voice questions
- 예전에 반응 좋았던 글 2~3개만 붙여줄 수 있어요?
- 평소에 반말로 써요, 존댓말로 써요?

## Mining the conversation first

Before asking, scan the current conversation. Real material often already exists:

- things the user built, installed, broke, or fixed
- exact words the user typed (complaints are gold: "뭔 소린지", "너무 무거운데")
- numbers that were actually counted
- what the assistant actually said back

Quote these accurately. Shorten, do not embellish.

## When the user refuses to give material

Write the draft with `[brackets]` around every invented detail, and say in one line that the brackets must be replaced with real details before posting.
