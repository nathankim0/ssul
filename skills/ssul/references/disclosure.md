# Sponsorship and AI disclosure (Korea)

Run on every post. If the user received anything for the post (money, free product, discount, points, affiliate commission, employment, revenue share), disclosure is a legal requirement in Korea, not a style choice. Basis: 공정거래위원회 「추천·보증 등에 관한 표시·광고 심사지침」 and its Q&A guide (「추천보증심사지침 안내서」). Rules change; when the stakes are real, tell the user to check the current text on the FTC site.

## Ask when unclear

If the post recommends a product, place, app, or service, and the request does not say, ask one question: "이 글 쓰는 대가로 받은 게 있어요? (돈, 제품, 할인, 수수료 등)". Do not guess.

## Where

- **Text posts (Threads, X, blog)**: in the **title or the first part** of the post, separated from the body and easy to notice. Not at the end, not in a comment, not behind "더보기" or a link. (End-of-post placement stopped being acceptable when the guideline was amended in late 2024.)
- **Photos**: inside the image, clearly visible against the background, or right next to it when photo and text read as one. As a hashtag, the **first** hashtag.
- **Video**: in the title or inside the video; if the whole video is sponsored, at the start, at the end, and repeated during it.
- In a Threads thread: the **first post**, not a later one.

## Wording

Say what was received, in Korean, without hedging.

| Received | Use |
|---|---|
| anything | 광고, 유료광고 |
| money | 광고비 지급, 금전적 지원, 수수료 지급 |
| free product or loan | OO 협찬, OO 무료 제공 |
| discount | OO 할인 지원 |
| points | OO 적립금 지급 |
| revenue share | 수익 일부 지급, 공동구매 |
| as a hashtag | #광고, #협찬 as the first hashtag |

Never use these as the disclosure: 체험단, 체험 후기, 서포터즈, 기자단, 선물, 지원 (without saying what), 초청, "#브랜드명", "브랜드명 감사합니다", "~에서 보내주셨어요", "받을 수 있음" and other conditional wording, English-only labels like "sponsored" or "collab".

"내돈내산" is fine only when the user really paid and received nothing afterwards (no refund, free trial, or affiliate link).

## How it fits the first line

Disclosure comes before the hook. Put it as a short separate first line (`[광고] OO 협찬`), then the hook on the next line. A slightly weaker first line is the price; never move the disclosure down to save the hook.

## AI-generated people

If a realistic AI-generated person recommends something in the post or image, say so in the first part ("AI로 생성한 가상인물이 포함된 게시물입니다"), and near the person in images. A virtual person must not claim experiences that did not happen.

## AI images in general

Realistic AI images (like ssul's image mode output) should carry the platform's AI label ("AI 정보" on Threads and Instagram).
