# Personas

A persona is who writes the post and who reads it. It sets the register range, the endings, how the story flows, whether hook formulas apply, laugh marks, the roll pool, and extra AI tells. The rest of ssul (real material, truth, disclosure, meaning-safe polish) is the same for every persona.

Real samples always beat these defaults. A persona is the starting point when samples are thin; once the user gives 3 or more real posts from the target writers, measure them (`analyze.md#voice-card`) and follow the samples wherever they differ from the table below.

## Picking the persona

In this order, the first that applies wins:

1. The user names it: "농민 말투", "농부처럼", "50대 농업인", "중장년 대상", "스레드 2030 감성".
2. The voice source: the user's own posts or pasted samples read like one persona.
3. The platform and topic: a farming community board or farming topic for farmers -> `farmer`; a 밴드, 카페, or 카카오스토리 for 40s and up -> `midlife`; Threads, X, Instagram -> `sns`.
4. Otherwise `sns`.

Say the persona in the notes. The user can switch it ("농민 버전으로도", "2030 말투로 바꿔").

## farmer, in more detail

- **Content first.** Posts are mostly practical: 정식, 약 친 날, 병해충, 서리, 비, 가뭄, 공판장 가격, 일손, 기계 고장, 몸 아픈 곳, and questions to other farmers. A plain ask ("[양파] 다들 언제 심으셨어요?") is a full post.
- **In order, no turn.** Tell the day the way it went. Do not add a 근데 pivot that turns an observation into a realization. "근데 해가 짧아진 게 이제 확실해요" is the young storytelling engine, not the way this reader writes.
- **Repetition is normal.** 했어요 / 했어요 / 했네요 / 좋겠네요 is how people write. Do not spread endings so each one is used once.
- **Mixed register is normal.** 해요체 lines with a 합니다 greeting or thanks in the same post (수고하셨습니다, 감사합니다) are natural here.
- **Not every line connects.** An aside about the weather or a sore back in the middle of a crop post is texture, not filler.
- **Address.** Use the board's own habit (회원님들, 선배님들, 농부님들, 다들) only when the samples or the user show it.
- **Spacing and punctuation.** Many real posts use loose spacing, no period at the end of a line, or `~~`. Mirror a habit only when it appears in at least half of the samples. Never invent misspellings.

What a farmer post's shape looks like, as placeholders, not a real post:

> 오늘 [아침에 영하]라 [하우스] 문 늦게 열었어요
> [고추] 정식 [절반]까지 했어요 나머지는 [내일] 하려고요
> [허리]가 좀 아프네요..
> 다들 [냉해] 조심하세요

## midlife, in more detail

Same rules as `farmer` for flow, endings, mixed register, and texture. Topics are daily: family, health, walks, food, hobbies, news. Greetings at the start or end (좋은 아침입니다, 오늘도 화이팅하세요) are common in some communities; use them only when the samples do.

## Extra AI tells for farmer and midlife

These are on top of `korean-voice.md#korean-ai-tells`. All are S1 for these personas; this overrides the S2 and S3 grades in `korean-voice.md#too-clean`.

| Tell | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Young 해요체 engine | ~거든요, ~더라고요, ~잖아요 stacked in one post | the plain endings the samples use (~했어요, ~네요, ~같아요) |
| Realization turn | "근데 해가 짧아진 게 이제 확실해요" | say what happened; drop the realization |
| Story arc | a setup, a 근데 turn, and a closing line that pays off | the day in order, stopping where it stops |
| Quotable last line | a punchline or a neat verdict | stop on the work, a wish, an ask, or a greeting |
| Too precise | 오전 6시 47분, 영하 2.3도, 정확히 37박스 | only the precision the material has; say it the way the speaker would |
| Evenly spread endings | every sentence ends differently | let the samples' endings repeat |
| Literary images | 가을이 깊어간다, 땅이 숨을 쉰다 | a concrete fact or nothing |
| Too clean | perfect spacing and periods when the samples have neither | match the samples' habits |
| Borrowed SNS words | 스친이들, 갓생, 킹받네, TMI | the community's own words |

## custom

When the user gives samples that fit none of the personas (a cooking community, a fishing club, a parents' group), build the persona from them: register mix, top endings and how often they repeat, laugh and trailing marks, emoji, typical first and last lines, line and post length, spacing habits. Write the result as a voice card (`analyze.md#voice-card`) and use it like a persona. Offer once to save it to `~/.ssul/voice.md` (`voice-bank.md`).
