# Content plan

Use when the user asks what to post ("뭐 올리지", "이번 주 계획", "아이디어 좀").

## Inputs

- the account's niche and audience (ask once if unknown)
- `~/.ssul/story-bank.md` and `voice.md` if they exist
- anything the user did, built, learned, or felt recently (the best source)
- the season and upcoming dates that matter to the niche (e.g. 김장철, 수능, 연말정산)

## Pillars

Pick 2 to 4 pillars the account returns to (e.g. for an AI developer: tool findings, failures, hot takes on news, behind the scenes). Every idea belongs to one.

## A week

5 to 7 ideas, mixed shapes so the feed does not repeat itself:

| Day | Pillar | Shape | Idea (one line) | Real material it needs | Have it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:

- at least 2 ideas the user can write today from material they already have
- at most 1 List and 1 Question per week
- every idea names the real material it needs; if the user does not have it, the idea says what to collect ("다음에 밭 나갈 때 온도계 사진 하나")
- no idea that depends on inventing an experience
- 0 or 1 trend-riding idea, only if the user has a genuine take on it

## Output

The table, then one line on which to write first and why. Offer to draft it right away.
